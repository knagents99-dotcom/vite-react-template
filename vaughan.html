Our story

Fifty years of the same hands, a generation of new ones.

Fiorio began on Avenue Road in 1973 — three brothers, three chairs, and a stubborn belief that a haircut should look better the morning after. Vaughan opened in 2009 to bring that same standard north of the city, with a space made for families, professionals, and anyone who values a stylist who actually listens.

Every senior stylist completes our in-house apprenticeship. We use Davines and Kérastase. We do not upsell. We do not rush colour. And we'll tell you honestly if a request won't suit your hair.
